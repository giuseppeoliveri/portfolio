About

I was born in Foggia, where I took my first steps into the world of creativity by earning a diploma in Graphic Design at Liceo Artistico Perugini.

I decided to continue my studies at the University of the Republic of San Marino (UNIRSM), where I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Design. This experience allowed me to sharpen my vision as a designer. During my career, I had the opportunity to intern at the LaTigre studio in Milan and work for a year at the RovaiWeber Design studio. These experiences helped me strengthen my professional skills and gain a deeper understanding of the design industry.

Currently, I am pursuing a second-level academic degree in Illustration at ISIA in Urbino. Here, I am further developing my artistic style and technical skills with the goal of establishing myself in the field of illustration and visual communication.
